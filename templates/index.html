Rounding: abs, ceil, floor, round, sign

Series: sum(...) (add all terms), prod(...) (multiply all terms)
Example: sum(sin(i*x) for i in range(5)) or prod(1 + i*x for i in range(1, 5))

Operators: x**2 (power), x/y (division)
